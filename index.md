# Privacy Policy — NotiSiren

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

NotiSiren is an Android application that turns notifications from apps you
choose into a loud, looping alarm. This policy explains how the app handles
your data.

## The short version

**NotiSiren collects no data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot
send anything anywhere.**

## What the app does with your notifications

NotiSiren reads incoming notifications using Android's official
NotificationListenerService interface. Notification contents are matched
locally, on your device, against the keywords you configure. Nothing is
uploaded, stored on a server, shared, or analyzed anywhere else.

## Permissions the app uses and why

- **Notification access** — required to read incoming notifications so the
  app can decide whether to raise an alarm. All processing happens on your
  device.
- **Alarm sound and vibration** — to ring the alarm.
- **Run in background / ignore battery optimizations** — requested at your
  explicit action so Android does not silence the alarm listener.
- **Read storage (Android 12 and older only)** — only to play a custom
  alarm tone you selected.

## Data we share

None. No advertising, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party services,
no network access.

## Data we store

Your settings (watched apps, keywords, chosen tones) are stored only in the
app's private storage on your device and never leave it. Uninstalling the
app removes everything.

## Children

NotiSiren is not directed at children under 13.
